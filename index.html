MT5 Setup

  1. Download the EA package below.
  2. Open MetaTrader 5 on your computer.
  3. Put the EA file in MQL5 → Experts.
  4. Restart MT5, open your chart, then attach the EA.
  5. Enable Algo Trading and test on a demo account first.
